- CTA in every video → **"Book a Discovery Call"**. No prices, no deposit amounts in
  any video: viewers may be in SA, the US or the UK. Pricing and deposits are handled on the call.

## The idea
Each client site already has a voice agent. That agent narrates a short video
presenting its own site. One site launched per day. Goal: fill the 2028 calendar.

## Video structure (30–45 s portrait, 60–90 s landscape)
1. **Hook (0–3 s)** — the agent speaks first: "Hi, I'm the voice of <site>. Let me show you around."
2. **CRM Solutions intro (3–6 s)** — one quiet card: "Built by CRM Solutions."
3. **The story (6–25 s)** — before/after screenshot, who the client is, what problem the site solves.
4. **Walkthrough (25–40 s)** — scrolling screen recording: booking, quote basket, voice agent live.
5. **Subtle CTA (last 3 s)** — "Want a site that talks for you? crmsolutions.app" — no hard sell.

## Formats
- Portrait 9:16 → WhatsApp Status (max 60 s per clip; ~30 s is safest), Facebook/Instagram Reels, TikTok.
- Landscape 16:9 → YouTube, LinkedIn.
- Always burned-in captions (most people watch muted).
