## E. Production notes

- **Sandy's voice:** the SCA-ZA site sets no voice name in code, so Sandy speaks in Gemini Live's default voice. For a consistent narration, generate the script with Gemini text-to-speech using that same voice, or record a real Sandy session and use her own lines. Pick one and use it for every SCA-ZA clip.
- **Honesty:** the midnight scene is framed as "picture it" storytelling. Do not show a fake enquiry from a real person or claim results the site has not had yet.
- **Permission:** get Neil's OK before posting (he is the client). One WhatsApp: "We made a short video showing off your new site — happy for us to share it?"
- **Assembly:** one template, two aspect ratios (Remotion or CapCut). Each later video swaps the narration, screenshots and closing line only.
- **Captions:** burned in, max two lines, high contrast, inside the platform safe area (top and bottom 15% clear on portrait).
- **Thumbnail / cover:** the share card at crmsolutions.app/work/sca-za/opengraph-image works as the YouTube/LinkedIn thumbnail.
