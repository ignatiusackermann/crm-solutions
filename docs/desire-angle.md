# The desire angle: rewriting Work pages and videos (18 Nov 2026)

## The principle (Caples 1926, Schwartz 1966)
- Caples sold piano lessons with "They laughed when I sat down at the piano, but
  when I started to play!" He sold the **moment**, not the lessons.
- Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*: copy cannot create desire. It can only
  take a desire that already exists in the reader and point it at the product.

## Schwartz tools we will use
1. **Mass desire.** Find the want the owner already has: more bookings without
   chasing, respect from their market, nights and weekends back, beating the
   bigger competitor. Features only appear as proof that the desire is met.
2. **Five levels of awareness.** A WhatsApp/Facebook viewer is mostly
   *problem-aware* ("my website brings nothing in"). So open with their
   frustration or wish, never with "CRM Solutions" or a feature list.
3. **Market sophistication.** SA owners have heard "you need a better website" a
   hundred times. Don't repeat that claim. Show the **new mechanism**: a site
   that talks, answers and books on its own, while the owner is busy.
4. **Intensification.** Make the viewer *see* it: "Picture it: it's 23:40, you're
   asleep, and a couple in Hartbeespoort has just checked your date..."
5. **Identification.** Speak to who they want to be: the firm people
   recommend, the agent trustees trust, the musician who is always booked.

## Honesty rule
Most sites are new. We never invent results. We use **"Picture it"** to describe
the imagined moment, and keep facts (counts, features) as proof under it.

## The owner's "moment" per site (draft — the Caples line for each)
The viewer is a **business owner**, so each moment is the owner's, not their customer's.
- **Saxophone for Functions:** He was on stage playing a first dance. By the
  last song, another couple had checked his date and sent a booking.
- **South Coast Advisory:** For 46 years, new clients came by referral. Now a
  business owner, worried about SARS at midnight, books a call before breakfast.
- **SCA Properties:** By the time the trustees sat down to meet, they had
  already read the proposal and made up their minds.
- **Yasuke Safety:** The mine's buyer sent last month's order again in two
  minutes, while the owner was still on the road to Tete.
- **Cabana Mio:** The family booked direct while the owner slept: no
  commission, no back-and-forth email.
- **Coastal Preferred Escapes:** A family understood a five-year membership in
  one visit, and signed up without a sales call.
- **Star Aesthetic:** Patients walk in already knowing the treatment they want,
  and trusting the clinic before they meet the doctor.
- **Shades of Blue:** Quote requests arrive complete. The owner prices them and
  sends them, without playing telephone tag.
- **Lava-SA / Storvac:** Buyers choose the right product themselves, so the
  owner stops explaining the same thing on the phone all day. *(Confirm with
  Ignatius the real pain for each.)*

## Video structure (replaces the earlier draft)
1. **The moment (0–5 s):** the voice agent sets the scene in the "picture it" style.
2. **The frustration (5–12 s):** what it was like before; the old site.
3. **The turn (12–30 s):** walk through the site as the answer, with features as proof.
4. **Identity (30–38 s):** who the owner gets to be now.
5. **CTA:** "Book a Discovery Call." No prices.
