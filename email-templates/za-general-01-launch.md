## Before this can send

The copy is finished and the lists are built. What is left is sending
infrastructure, not content.

### The lists (built 2 Sept 2026)

Sourced from the biz-accelerator directory export (2,147 category workbooks,
~316,000 SA companies) via `scripts/build-za-lists.py`. Every row has an address
on the business's **own domain** — free and ISP mailboxes are dropped, because
those are a person rather than a business and they weaken both deliverability
and the POPIA position. One contact per domain. Role addresses (info@, admin@,
reception@) are sorted to the front, so `batch-01` is always the strongest batch
to warm on.

| Category | Contacts | Batches | Fit with this email |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Accountant | 1,533 | 6 | **Strongest.** Recurring annual clients — the second sale *is* their model. 51% role addresses. |
| Security | 1,265 | 5 | **Strong.** Monitoring and guarding are contract revenue. 61% role, 87% have a website. |
| Cleaning | 842 | 3 | **Strong.** Contract recurring. 59% role, 86% have a website. |
| Restaurants | 1,064 | 4 | **Good.** Repeat custom is the whole game — but thin margins against R20,000. |
| Legal Practitioners & Attorneys | 2,049 | 7 | **Largest.** Repeat and referral driven, budget exists, but a heavily marketed-to audience. |

**6,753 contacts — 23 days at 300 a day.** Foreign firms (UK, Australia) that
had leaked into the source directory are filtered out: the footer states the
recipient is a South African business, so that has to be true.

**Start with Accountant** — see `00-SEND-SHEET-accountant.md` for the ramp.

Accommodation is **on hold**: 3,010 batched plus a 6,771 remaining pool already

on gmail or share one practice domain.

### Before batch one goes out

1. **Authenticate crm-solutions.online.** SPF, DKIM and a DMARC record, all
   verified before a single send. A brand-new domain with no authentication is
   the fastest route to a spam folder there is.
2. **Warm the domain slowly.** 300 a day is a sensible ceiling, but do not start
   there on day one — 50, then 100, then 200, then 300, watching bounces and
   complaints at each step. Stop and diagnose if bounces exceed 5%.
3. **Keep the sending domain and the site domain separate but aligned.** Mail
   goes from crm-solutions.online; every link points to www.crmsolutions.app.
   That is fine, but the From name and the footer must make it obvious the two
   are the same business, or it reads like a spoof.
4. **Confirm the reply-to mailbox is monitored.** The call to action is the
   calculator; the conversion is the reply. An unread inbox wastes the campaign.
5. **Suppression list across all categories.** One unsubscribe must remove that
   address from every future category send, not just the one it came from.

## Send notes

- **`[COMPANY]`** must exist as a PHPList attribute mapped to the CSV `company`
  column, with a default of "your business" so it never renders blank.
- **No images and no tracking pixel on campaign one.** Both cost inbox placement
  while the domain has no sending history.
- **No `[FIRSTNAME]`.** Unless the list carries verified first names, a blank or
  wrong greeting is worse than none.
- **No price anywhere.** The landing page deliberately carries no figure and
  defers to the written proposal. Do not put R20,000 in the email.
- The email makes **no claim of having looked at their website.** At 300 an hour
  that would not be true, and owners recognise the pattern instantly.
- The unsubscribe link and the identifying footer are not optional under POPIA
  s69. Keep both in the text and the HTML part.
- Reply handling matters more than opens here.
- **Rebuild or extend a list** with:
  `python scripts/build-za-lists.py "Accountant" --out <data-002 dir>`
  Add `--probe` to see the yield without writing anything.
